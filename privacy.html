Sea Service Logger

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Summary: Sea Service Logger stores all your data locally on your device. In version 1, nothing is sent to any server. Your sea service records, vessel details, and location data never leave your phone unless you choose to export a PDF.

1. Who we are

Sea Service Logger is operated as an independent application. For privacy enquiries, contact: privacy@seaservicelogger.com

This policy covers the Sea Service Logger Android application and any associated web application hosted at the same domain.

2. What data we collect and why

The app collects only what you enter and what your device provides:

  • Mariner profile — your name and MMC/CoC number. Used to pre-fill generated documents. Stored locally.
  • Vessel information — vessel name, official number, GRT, propulsion, flag, and captain contact details. Used to generate compliant sea service documents. Stored locally.
  • Daily log entries — date, vessel, status (underway/at anchor), waters type, hours, and optional location. This is the core record the app exists to maintain. Stored locally.
  • Device location (GPS) — optionally used to auto-fill the location field when you log a day. The app requests your location only when you open the log entry form. Raw coordinates are stored in your local log entry if captured. No continuous background tracking occurs.

We do not collect: payment information, biometric data, browsing history, contacts, photos, or any data not listed above.

3. How your data is stored

In version 1, all data is stored exclusively in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This means:

  • Data does not leave your device over the network
  • Clearing your browser/app data will permanently delete your records — back up via PDF export before doing so
  • Data is not encrypted at rest in v1 (it is in plain text in device storage)
  • Data is not shared between devices

4. Third-party services

When you export a PDF document, the app loads the jsPDF library from a CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). This is a one-time network request at export time. No personal data is included in this request — only the library file is fetched. Cloudflare's privacy policy governs this request.

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services are active in v1.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party, except as explicitly initiated by you — for example, when you email a generated PDF to your captain.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access — all your data is visible within the app at all times
  • Rectification — edit any entry directly in the app
  • Erasure — delete individual entries or all data by clearing app storage
  • Portability — export your records as PDF at any time

Because v1 stores data only on your device, exercising these rights is entirely within your control. No request to us is needed.

7. Children

The app is intended for maritime professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 16 years of age.

8. Future versions (cloud sync)

A planned future version will offer optional cloud sync (Pro tier). If and when that feature launches, this privacy policy will be updated before activation, and you will be asked to consent to the new terms. Cloud sync will always be opt-in.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. Material changes will be notified in-app.
